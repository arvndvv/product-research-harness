# Domain guide: mirrorless and DSLR cameras

Aliases: mirrorless, mirrorless camera, dslr, interchangeable lens camera, vlogging camera

No measurement table: camera quality depends on the lens, settings and use, and lab databases don't
cover all current bodies the same way. Cameras are compared side by side on reviews and samples.

## Decision factors

- **Main use**: travel, people/portraits, sports and wildlife, video/vlogging, low light, learning photography.
- **Sensor size**: Micro Four Thirds, APS-C, full frame; bigger sensors help low light and background blur, but cost and weigh more.
- **Lens system and budget**: the lenses the person needs (kit zoom, prime, telephoto) and their cost; the system's lens range and third-party lenses.
- **Autofocus**: subject/eye tracking for people, animals and fast action.
- **Video**: resolution and frame rates, recording limits and overheating, log profiles, microphone/headphone jacks, flip screen for vlogging.
- **Stabilisation**: in-body image stabilisation (IBIS) for handheld video and low light.
- **Handling and size**: grip, weight, viewfinder quality, weather sealing.
- **Battery life**: shots per charge, USB-C charging.
- **Used market**: used bodies and lenses are good value for some systems.

## What actually matters

- **Lenses outlast bodies**: the system and its lenses matter more than one body's specs.
- **Autofocus and stabilisation** now separate cameras more than megapixels.
- **For vlogging**, a flip screen, good audio input and stabilisation matter more than sensor size.

## Sources

- In-depth camera reviewers (DPReview's archive and similar, Gerald Undone and other specialists for video).
- Sample images and videos in the conditions the person cares about.
- Owner communities (Reddit r/photography, r/videography, system-specific subreddits) for reliability and lens advice.

## Pitfalls

- **Megapixels** don't decide image quality.
- **Video recording limits and overheating** vary a lot between bodies; check tests.
- **Grey-market imports** may have no local warranty.

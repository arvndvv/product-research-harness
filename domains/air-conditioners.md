# Domain guide: air conditioners

Aliases: air conditioner, ac, split ac, window ac, inverter ac

Written for India-style split and window ACs. ISEER is India's standardised seasonal efficiency
rating (on the BEE star label); for other countries use the local equivalent (SEER, CSPF).

## Decision factors

- **Room size and heat load**: tonnage (roughly 1 ton up to ~120 sq ft, 1.5 ton up to ~180 sq ft, 2 ton larger rooms; more for top floors, sunny rooms, many people).
- **Inverter vs fixed speed**: inverter ACs save power and hold temperature better.
- **Energy efficiency**: BEE star rating and ISEER value; running cost matters more than purchase price over years.
- **Split vs window**: split is quieter and more efficient; window is cheaper to install.
- **Condenser coil**: copper (easier to repair, durable) vs aluminium; anti-corrosion coating near the sea.
- **Cooling at high outdoor temperatures**: rated performance at 50°C+ ambient for hot regions.
- **Noise**: indoor unit noise for bedrooms.
- **Features**: convertible capacity modes, PM2.5 filter, Wi-Fi control, dehumidification, auto-clean.
- **Installation and service**: installation charges and kit, service network, warranty (compressor warranty is usually longer).

## What actually matters

- **Right tonnage first**: an undersized AC runs constantly and never cools; oversized wastes money.
- **ISEER compares running cost directly**: higher ISEER uses less power for the same cooling.
- **Service network quality** in the person's city matters as much as the brand.

## Sources

- The BEE star label and the maker's spec sheet for ISEER and power consumption.
- Owner reviews and regional forums for service experience, installation quality and noise.
- Consumer testing organisations where available for the person's country.

## Pitfalls

- **Star ratings change over time** (BEE revises the table); compare ISEER values, not stars across years.
- **Installation extras** (copper pipe length, stand, stabiliser) add to the price.
- **"5-in-1 convertible" modes** reduce capacity; they don't add cooling.

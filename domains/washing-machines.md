# Domain guide: washing machines

Aliases: washing machine, washer, front load washer, top load washer, washer dryer

No measurement table: independent wash-performance tests aren't widely published in the same way
for most markets. Machines are compared side by side on reviews and the specs that decide fit.

## Decision factors

- **Type**: front load (better wash, less water, gentler) vs top load (cheaper, faster, easier to load); semi-automatic vs fully automatic.
- **Capacity**: roughly 6-7 kg for 1-3 people, 8 kg for 4-5, 9 kg+ for larger families or bedding.
- **Water and power**: water pressure at home (top loaders and front loaders have minimums), hard water (in-built heater helps), energy and water use.
- **Wash programs**: quick wash, heavy, delicates, allergen/steam; in-built heater for hot washes.
- **Spin speed**: higher spin dries clothes faster (matters in monsoon).
- **Motor**: inverter/direct-drive motors are quieter and usually carry longer motor warranties.
- **Dryer**: washer-dryer combos save space but dry slowly and add complexity.
- **Size and placement**: footprint, door opening space, bathroom or balcony installation.
- **Service network and warranty** in the person's city.

## What actually matters

- **Front loaders clean better and use less water**; top loaders suit people who want speed or have low water pressure.
- **Hard water areas benefit from a heater** and correct detergent.
- **Service quality** decides how painful a breakdown is.

## Sources

- Consumer testing organisations where available (they test wash performance, rinse quality, noise).
- Owner reviews and regional forums for reliability, service and noise.

## Pitfalls

- **Capacity is dry-weight**; a "7 kg" drum is for normal cotton loads, less for delicates.
- **Water pressure requirements** can cause errors in homes with low pressure.
- **Installation on uneven floors** causes vibration and noise.

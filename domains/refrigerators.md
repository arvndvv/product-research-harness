# Domain guide: refrigerators

Aliases: refrigerator, fridge, double door fridge, side by side fridge

No measurement table: energy use depends on capacity and type, so label figures only compare
fairly between fridges of similar size and type. They're compared side by side instead.

## Decision factors

- **Capacity for the household**: roughly 190-250 L for 1-2 people, 250-350 L for 3-4, 350 L+ for larger families or bulk shopping.
- **Type**: single door (direct cool), double door (frost free), triple door, side-by-side, French door; freezer position (top or bottom).
- **Direct cool vs frost free**: direct cool is cheaper and efficient but needs manual defrosting.
- **Energy use**: BEE star rating and annual consumption (kWh/year) on the label, compared within similar capacity.
- **Inverter compressor**: quieter, more efficient, better with voltage fluctuations; compressor warranty length.
- **Power cuts and voltage**: stabiliser-free operation range, cooling retention during power cuts.
- **Layout**: shelves, vegetable crisper, convertible freezer modes, water dispenser.
- **Size and fit**: width, height, door swing, kitchen space.
- **Service network** in the person's city.

## What actually matters

- **Right capacity and layout for the household** matter more than features.
- **Energy use over 10+ years** adds up; compare annual kWh within the same size and type.
- **Compressor warranty and service quality** reflect long-term reliability.

## Sources

- The BEE label (India) or local energy label for annual consumption.
- Consumer testing organisations where available; owner reviews for noise, cooling consistency and service.

## Pitfalls

- **Gross vs usable capacity**: listed litres include space you can't really use.
- **Star ratings change** with BEE revisions; compare kWh/year instead.
- **Smart features** add cost and rarely matter.

# Domain guide: wireless earbuds

Aliases: earbud, tws, true wireless, airpods, in-ear

Expert guidance used in every prompt when the category mentions earbuds. Edit freely. The "Decision
factors" list drives the interview; the "Measurements" table seeds the measurements and scales.

## Decision factors

- **Sound quality**: tuning (bass-heavy vs balanced), EQ app, codec support (AAC, LDAC, aptX) for the person's phone.
- **Noise cancelling (ANC)**: how much it blocks commutes, offices and flights; transparency mode quality.
- **Call quality**: microphone performance in wind and traffic, not just in a quiet room.
- **Fit and comfort**: ear-tip sizes, stem vs bud shape, comfort for long wear, stability for workouts.
- **Battery life**: hours per charge with ANC on, plus total with the case.
- **Connection**: Bluetooth version, multipoint (two devices at once), latency for videos and games.
- **Water and sweat resistance**: IP rating for workouts and rain.
- **App and controls**: touch or button controls, find-my features, firmware updates.
- **Phone ecosystem**: some features only work with the same brand's phone (e.g. AirPods with iPhone, Galaxy Buds with Samsung).
- **Durability and after-sales service**: case hinge, battery ageing, warranty claims in the person's country.

## What actually matters

- **Measured ANC and sound** differ a lot more than spec sheets suggest; "ANC up to 50 dB" claims are not comparable between brands.
- **Fit decides both comfort and sound**: a poor seal ruins bass and ANC. Owner reports on fit are valuable.
- **Budget brands** (common in India: boAt, Noise, realme, OnePlus Nord, CMF) are rarely lab-tested.
  When no measurements exist for every product, they're compared side by side on reviews instead.
- **Codecs only matter if the phone supports them**: LDAC helps on many Android phones, never on iPhone.

## Sources

- RTINGS: lab-measured sound, noise isolation, microphone and battery for widely sold models.
- SoundGuys and specialist audio reviewers for tuning and microphone samples.
- Owner communities (Reddit r/headphones, r/IndianGaming for budget buds, brand forums) for fit, connection drops and durability.
- Retailer reviews in the person's country for after-sales service experience.

## Pitfalls

- **Look-alike listings**: search results often mix in clones "for" a brand-name model. Confirm the seller and exact model.
- **Total battery claims** include the case; compare hours per charge with ANC on.
- **Generations**: "2", "2 Pro", "3" can differ completely in ANC and fit.
- **Regional variants** may lack features (e.g. LDAC, certain app features).

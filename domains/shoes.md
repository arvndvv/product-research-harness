# Domain pack: shoes

Aliases: shoe, sneakers, trainers, running shoes

Expert guidance used in every prompt when the category mentions shoes. Edit freely,
because this file is how you teach the harness a domain. Copy it to make `laptops.md`,
`headphones.md` and so on.

## Decision factors

- **Fit and width**: foot length and width, toe box room, true-to-size or not.
- **Use**: running, walking all day, gym/court, trail, or a mix.
- **Cushioning**: soft vs firm, stack height.
- **Heel-to-toe drop**: what the person is used to.
- **Stability**: neutral vs stability support.
- **Grip**: surfaces (road, turf, wet floors).
- **Weight**: matters for running.
- **Durability**: outsole and upper wear for the person's mileage.
- **Breathability**: hot and humid climates.

## What actually matters

- **Fit beats everything.** Length, width (forefoot/midfoot), toe box shape and volume, heel hold.
  Wide or high-volume feet should look for explicit wide (2E/4E) versions or roomy toe boxes.
  Owner reports about "runs small / narrow / true to size" are high-value evidence.
- **Use case decides the category.** Daily training, tempo/race, trail, walking/standing all day,
  gym/court. A great race shoe is a poor daily shoe and vice versa.
- **Cushioning and stack height**: soft vs firm, how it feels after weeks (foams can pack out).
- **Heel-to-toe drop** (mm): matters for people used to a specific drop or with calf/Achilles sensitivity.
- **Stability vs neutral**: guidance rails, wider bases, posts. Only needed if the person reports overpronation or prior issues with neutral shoes.
- **Weight** for running use.
- **Durability**: outsole rubber coverage, upper tearing, midsole lifespan. Owner reports from Reddit/forums are the best source; reviewers rarely test past 100 km.
- **Grip**: wet traction matters a lot in rainy climates.
- **Breathability**: important in hot, humid climates.

## Sources

- Lab-style testing with measurements (stack, drop, weight, width, softness) is the strongest expert source.
- Specialist running/shoe review sites that test over many kilometres beat general tech/lifestyle listicles.
- Reddit communities for running and running shoes: look for threads from people with similar feet or use.
- Brand sites for official specs and available widths; local retailers for real price and sizes in stock.

## Pitfalls

- Model generations change fit and foam (e.g. "v12" vs "v13"); confirm the version.
- Sizes differ across brands and between UK/US/EU sizing; note which system the person uses.
- Regional pricing and availability vary a lot; an option not sold in the person's region (or only at a big import markup) is a weak recommendation.

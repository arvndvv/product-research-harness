# Domain guide: smartwatches and fitness trackers

Aliases: smartwatch, smart watch, fitness band, fitness tracker, sports watch, gps watch

No measurement table: there's no widely published, comparable lab measurement for watches yet.
Products are compared side by side on reviews; battery life claims are manufacturer figures.

## Decision factors

- **Phone compatibility**: Apple Watch needs an iPhone; Wear OS and many fitness watches work with Android (and some with iPhone with limits).
- **Main use**: notifications and daily wear, fitness and sleep tracking, serious running/cycling with GPS, health features.
- **Battery life**: real days of use with the features the person will use (always-on display and GPS cut it sharply).
- **Fitness and health accuracy**: heart rate, GPS track accuracy, sleep tracking; in-depth reviews test these against chest straps and reference devices.
- **Display**: AMOLED vs MIP/transflective (readable in sun, longer battery), size, brightness.
- **Smart features**: apps, payments (NFC, check availability in the person's country), calls from the wrist, LTE versions.
- **Fit and comfort**: case size, weight, strap options, comfort for sleep tracking.
- **Durability**: water rating for swimming (5 ATM+), screen protection.
- **Subscriptions**: some brands lock features or coaching behind a paid plan.
- **After-sales service**: warranty and service centres in the person's country.

## What actually matters

- **Ecosystem first**: the person's phone narrows the choice more than anything else.
- **Accuracy varies widely**, especially among budget fitness bands and budget smartwatches; in-depth reviewers who compare against reference devices are the evidence, not spec sheets.
- **Battery claims assume light use**: compare battery with the features the person wants (GPS hours, always-on display).

## Sources

- In-depth testers who compare heart-rate and GPS against reference devices (e.g. DC Rainmaker, The Quantified Scientist) for fitness accuracy.
- General reviewers for smart features and battery in daily use.
- Owner communities (Reddit r/AppleWatch, r/GalaxyWatch, r/Garmin, brand forums) for long-term battery, strap and software issues.

## Pitfalls

- **Look-alike budget watches** copy premium designs; check brand, app quality and reviews.
- **Health features vary by country** (ECG, blood pressure features may be disabled in some regions).
- **"Up to 14 days" battery** usually means with most features off.

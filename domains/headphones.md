# Domain guide: headphones (over-ear and on-ear)

Aliases: headphone, over-ear, on-ear, wireless headphones, anc headphones, headset

Scales are rules of thumb for late 2026.

## Decision factors

- **Use**: commuting and flights (ANC), office calls, home listening, studio/monitoring, gaming.
- **Wireless or wired**: Bluetooth convenience vs wired quality and latency; codec support (AAC, LDAC, aptX) for the person's phone.
- **Noise cancelling**: how much it blocks; transparency mode quality.
- **Sound quality and tuning**: bass-heavy vs balanced; EQ app.
- **Comfort**: weight, clamping force, ear cup size, heat for long wear, glasses compatibility.
- **Call quality**: microphone in noisy places.
- **Battery life**: hours with ANC on.
- **Connection**: multipoint, latency for videos and games.
- **Portability**: folding design, case.
- **Durability and service**: hinge and headband cracks are a common failure; warranty in the person's country.

## What actually matters

- **Comfort decides whether they get used**: owner reports about clamping and heat are valuable.
- **ANC strength differs a lot between models** and is measurable.
- **Studio headphones** are tuned flat and usually wired, without ANC: a different category from commuter headphones.

## Sources

- RTINGS for measured frequency response, noise isolation, battery and latency.
- SoundGuys and specialist audio reviewers; Crinacle and audiophile communities for wired/studio models.
- Owner communities (Reddit r/headphones, brand forums) for comfort, cracking headbands and firmware problems.

## Pitfalls

- **Generations** ("XM4" vs "XM5" vs "XM6") differ in fit, ANC and folding.
- **Fake listings** of popular models exist on marketplaces; buy from authorised sellers.
- **Codec support depends on the phone**: LDAC/aptX don't work with iPhones.
